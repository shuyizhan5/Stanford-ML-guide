What QuantFormer Does

At a high level, the model takes historical stock-market sequences, passes them through a Transformer, produces prediction scores, ranks stocks cross-sectionally, and then uses those rankings to form a portfolio.

Historical stock sequences ↓ Transformer / Attention ↓ Prediction scores ↓ Cross-sectional ranking ↓ Top-ranked stocks ↓ Portfolio / Backtest

How to Think About Attention in Quant

In a financial time series, attention can be viewed as a dynamic information-selection mechanism. Instead of treating every historical timestep equally, the model can learn which past market states are more relevant to the current prediction.

For example, the model may assign more importance to recent volatility spikes, unusual turnover, regime shifts, or older periods that resemble the current market state.

Intuition: attention asks, “Which parts of the historical sequence matter most for the prediction I am making now?”

What to Check in a Real Quant Study

Target

What exactly is the model predicting: return, ranking score, volatility, or something else?

Data Leakage

Make sure no future information enters features, labels, normalization, or portfolio construction.

Out-of-Sample

Evaluate the strategy on unseen periods with realistic transaction costs and walk-forward testing.
